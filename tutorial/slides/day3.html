⚠️ The trap nobody escapes

r = urequests.get(url)
data = r.json()
# DO NOT FORGET:
r.close()

After ~10 unclosed responses, your board crashes. Use:

r = urequests.get(url)
try:
    data = r.json()
finally:
    r.close()
