Day 5 — Ship It 🚀

The goal: learn one pro concept (two ways to write the same program — sync vs async), then build your own feature and demo the whole smart house to your family. This is graduation day. 🎓

📖 Field guide: day5.html. Today is lighter on new stuff and heavier on making it yours — the final project is whatever you think the house should do.

🎯 Missions

  • ☐ M1 · Two shapes, same behavior. Do Part 1 — flip USE_ASYNC=True. ✅ Win 5.1: the board behaves exactly the same with async on.
  • ☐ M2 · Spot the difference. Do Part 2 and Part 3 — when would you pick each?

🧠 Full-circle moment

Remember your Code Week Wednesday boss — the little "smart house" you built in Python with rooms, devices, and typed commands? Open the real firmware (simple_led.py) next to it. Same idea: register, loop, check state, switch a light. You basically wrote the firmware a week early — you just didn't have the board yet. 😎

👑 Boss Build — your own feature + the demo

Do Part 4 — Final project. Invent something the house does that isn't in the tutorial. Ideas: a "movie mode" that dims things, a doorbell that flashes the LED, a counter of how many times motion fired today, a new dashboard button — your call.

✅ Win 5.2: your feature works end-to-end.

🏆 Final milestone (5.3): the demo. Do the wrap-up — gather the family and, in 2 minutes, show your smart house and explain what it does and how the board and hub talk. This is the finale — make it an event.

💬 Explain-Back / Demo (2 min — the big one)

  • In kid words: what's the difference between sync and async? When would you use each?
  • Demo your feature. What does it do, and which pieces (board, WiFi, hub, dashboard) does it use?
  • What was the hardest bug this week, and how did you figure it out? (Engineers love this question.)

💵 Payday: Missions + Boss = base $25 · Explain-Back/Demo = +$20 · stretch or a polished demo = +$5. Plus the 🏆 finale bonus if the family demo goes off — your manager's call.

🎓 You did it. You built a real Internet-of-Things device from scratch — sensor, WiFi, web dashboard, alarm — and you understand how every piece talks to the others. When you walk into camp, you won't be learning this for the first time; you'll be the engineer who already knows. Go show off. 🚀
