🧑‍🏫 Manager's Guide — Dry Run

This is the same light-touch playbook as Code Week, adapted for the real hardware camp she's doing solo at home. It has the daily answer key, the Explain-Back prompts, the one prep job, and how to help without giving it away.

🧭 Her frame this week: "junior counselor"

She's not just a student — she's acting as a junior counselor validating the camp's lab content: does every assignment actually work, and do the days stay consistent? That framing does real work for you: it gives her permission to be critical, a reason to read carefully, and pride in catching problems instead of hiding when she's stuck. Encourage her Counselor's Logbook (doable? consistent? broken/confusing?), and treat a genuine bug she finds in the material as an easy Extra-mile ($5) — that's exactly a counselor's job. (Anything real she logs, send it my way and it gets fixed.)

💬 The Explain-Back (same as last week)

Before she collects her understanding bonus, she spends 2 minutes doing two of: explain a piece of her code, predict what a line/click will do, or make a tiny live change you ask for. If she did the work, she nails it; if she copied, she can't. Understanding can't be faked out loud.

🔧 Your one prep job

This week is more physical than Code Week. Before each day, make sure she has:

  • The hub runnable — she starts it herself (python app.py in implementation/iot_hub), but confirm Python/PyCharm work.
  • 2.4 GHz WiFi she can name and the password. (The ESP32 can't see 5 GHz — this trips up almost everyone on Day 3.)
  • A good USB data cable and ideally a rear USB port or a powered hub. A weak cable is the #1 cause of Day 3 grief (brownouts).
📚 Your deep-help resource: the camp's own counsellor guides — day1, day2, day3, day4, day5 — each has "Where they'll get stuck," "Ready-to-paste hints," and "What done looks like." That's your cheat sheet if she's stuck and you want the exact fix.

🧑‍🔧 Day 4 solo note (important)

The global alarm needs two armed houses. She only has one board, so she registers a fake second house from the dashboard console (a fetch POST, exactly like Day 1) and arms both. When she waves at the real house, the dashboard should flag the fake one as in alarm too — that proves the "all armed houses" logic even though the fake house has no physical buzzer. A borrowed second board is a nice bonus but not required.

🪜 Helping without giving it away

  1. "Read the last line of the error out loud. What's it saying?"
  2. "What did you expect, and what happened instead?"
  3. On hardware days: "Check the cable and the pin before the code — is it seated? right port?"
  4. "Try the ▶ Peek on the mission page, or the matching Part on the field guide — then do it from memory."

Resist typing on her keyboard. The goal is her "aha," not a working file.

🚩 Signs she's pasting instead of thinking

  • Can't explain what her own code does, or goes quiet during the Explain-Back.
  • Code is fancier than the field guide taught, with features she can't account for.
  • Finishes with zero errors along the way (real hardware work has messy middles — loose wires, WiFi sulks).
  • Fumbles a 60-second live change.

If you see these: no drama — "Cool, walk me through it," then do a live change together. If she can't, that day's understanding bonus waits until she can explain it. Coaching, not a courtroom.
