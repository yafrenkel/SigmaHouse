🧭 Dry Run: Junior Counselor

Congratulations — you got promoted. 🎉 Last week you were a Junior Developer (Code Week). You crushed it, so this week you level up to Junior Counselor: you'll build a real Internet-of-Things smart house — a tiny WiFi computer (the ESP32) talking to a web dashboard — but this time as a tester, not just a student. 🤖

You are now acting as a junior counselor. The camp's hands-on lab content (the "semilab") needs a real test-run before the campers arrive — and that's your job. You'll validate it: build every project yourself, confirm each assignment is actually doable, and check that the days fit together and stay consistent. Anything broken, confusing, or out of order — you catch it and log it. Do the work → get paid. Understand it → earn the bonus. (See The Paycheck.)

How this week works — you have TWO tabs open each day:
  • 📋 This mission page (e.g. "Day 1") — your checklist, your Boss build, your rewards, and the exact snags to watch for.
  • 📖 The camp field guide (day1.html … day5.html) — the real instructions and code. Your mission page links straight to it.

Icons you'll see: 🎯 Mission (a task) · ✅ Win (a milestone — check it off) · 🐞 Snag (a common problem + the fix) · 👑 Boss (the day's build) · ▶ Peek (a hint, hidden until you click).

🧭 Your counselor mission — validate as you go

You're not just doing the assignments, you're checking them. As you work each day, keep a short Counselor's Logbook (a notes file or paper) and jot:

  • ✅ Doable? Did each mission actually work by following the field guide?
  • 🔗 Consistent? Did today build cleanly on yesterday — nothing out of order, nothing that needs a step you weren't taught yet?
  • 🐞 Broken or confusing? A wrong instruction, a dead link, a typo, or any bit that made you go "huh?"

Bring your notes to clock-out. Finding a real bug in the camp content is a win — that's exactly what a counselor is for. 🕵️

🧰 What you need set up

  • 💻 Thonny (for the ESP32) and PyCharm (to run the hub) — same tools as camp.
  • 🤖 The ESP32 kit and a good USB data cable (a weak cable causes half the problems — see Day 3).
  • 📶 2.4 GHz WiFi — the ESP32 cannot see 5 GHz networks.
  • 🌐 New to any word? Keep the Glossary, Circuits, and PWM pages open in a tab.

💡 Rules of a good engineer

  • Read the last line of the error. Red text is normal — it usually tells you exactly what's wrong.
  • Change one thing at a time. When something breaks, undo your last change first.
  • Check the wire before the code. On hardware days, half of all bugs are a loose cable or wrong pin.
  • Type it, don't paste it. Your fingers learn too.
  • Stuck 10 min? Walk, then Peek. That's smart, not cheating.
