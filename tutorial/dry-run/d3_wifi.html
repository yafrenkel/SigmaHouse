Day 3 — Get Online 📶

The goal: the big one. Your board joins the WiFi, finds the hub, and appears in the dashboard. Then you close the loop: click the dashboard → the real LED changes; press the board's button → the dashboard changes. This is the day the two halves of the system finally talk.

📖 Field guide: day3.html. Both the hub (from Day 1) and the board need to be running today. Heads-up: WiFi is the fussiest part of the whole week — the snag box below has the exact fixes, so don't panic if it doesn't connect on the first try. Real engineers expect this.

🐞 Snags to watch for (WiFi day = expect a couple)

▶ "Connection lost / Access is denied" the moment it prints "Connecting to WiFi…"

That's a power brownout: the WiFi radio spikes the current and the weak USB link resets. Fix the power: short good data cable, straight into a rear USB port (no hub), or a powered USB hub. Then Stop/Restart in Thonny.

▶ OSError: Wifi Internal Error

The radio got wedged (often right after a brownout reset). Power-cycle the board — unplug USB, wait 3 seconds, replug (or press EN/RST). The connect code then resets the radio and retries on its own, so re-run it.

▶ It never finds your network

The ESP32 only sees 2.4 GHz WiFi, never 5 GHz. If your network has one name for both, connect a phone to the 2.4 GHz one to confirm the password, then double-check WIFI_SSID / WIFI_PASS (exact spelling and case).

▶ It connects but the house never appears

The board can't reach the hub. Check three things: the hub is running (python app.py), your HUB_URL uses your laptop's real IP + :8080, and the laptop and board are on the same WiFi.

▶ Keep your WiFi password private

Your real SSID/password live in secrets.py only — never put them in files you push to GitHub. (The push script even checks for this.)
