Day 4 — Sensors & Alarm 🚨

The goal: give your house a sense — a motion sensor — and build the coolest feature of the week: the global alarm. Wave your hand at one armed house and every armed house sounds off. Then you'll add a "panic mode" that arms or disarms them all with one click.

🧑‍🔧 Solo setup for the two-house alarm

The global alarm needs two armed houses, but you have one board. Two ways to do it:

  • Easiest: keep your real board running (House A), then register a fake House B from the dashboard console the same way you did on Day 1 (a fetch POST with a made-up unique_id). Arm both. Now wave at your real House A → watch the dashboard mark House B in alarm too. Your real house buzzes; the fake one lights up red in the dashboard (it has no buzzer, but the hub still flags it — that's the whole point).
  • Even better (if you can): borrow a second board (a parent or friend with the kit) so you hear two buzzers. Optional bonus, not required.

🐞 Snags to watch for

▶ The PIR's own little light seems backwards

The tiny indicator LED on the sensor module is just cosmetic and can look "reversed." Ignore it — the dashboard (and pir.value()) is the real source of truth. If the dashboard reacts when you wave, it's working.

▶ "Motion" shows and never clears

By design, a motion reading auto-clears after a few seconds so one wave doesn't latch forever. If it seems stuck, wait ~3 seconds and check again.

▶ Nothing buzzes even though motion is detected

Remember the two conditions: the house that reports motion must be armed, AND the house you expect to buzz must also be armed. Arm both, then wave.
